Some welcome news to close out the year: I was named a Finalist for BrightSign's 2025 Graham Impact Awards, a company-wide award run by Graham Partners, BrightSign's private equity backer, recognising contribution to the business.

It landed at a good point in the year. I'd been promoted to Director of Software Engineering not long before, now leading the Cloud, Platform and Client teams behind the BSN.Cloud SaaS platform and BrightSign Control Plus, and this was a nice bit of external recognition for the work those teams have been putting in.

None of it happens solo. Thanks to everyone across Cloud, Platform and Client for the work that made this possible.
